# Phase 10: Historical Validation & Backtesting of the Broad NSE EQ Research Engine

## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose
Phase 10 provides a rigorous, point-in-time **Historical Validation & Backtesting Framework** for the Wyckoff Stock Screener Research Engine. It evaluates whether candidate signals (`HIGH_PRIORITY_CANDIDATE`, `QUALIFIED_CANDIDATE`, `WATCHLIST`, `NO_SETUP`, `DISQUALIFIED`) and mechanical qualification gates generated by the Research Engine at historical checkpoints demonstrate measurable, statistically sound forward outperformance over 10-, 20-, and 60-trading-day horizons.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Strict Non-Optimization & No-Fabrication Principle**:
> This is a **pure empirical validation layer**. It does **NOT** modify, retune, optimize, or fit any analytical thresholds, scoring formulas, VSA parameters, or mechanical qualification rules to improve historical return figures. All underlying analytical logic from Phases 8, 9A, 9B, and 9C remains 100% frozen.

---

## 2. Point-in-Time Architecture & Anti-Leakage Safeguards

```
[Start Date]──────────────────────────────[Checkpoint Bar T]───────[T+10]───[T+20]───[T+60]
 ├──────────────────────────────────────────────┤ ├────────────────────────────────────┤
            SIGNAL GENERATION WINDOW                           OUTCOME MEASUREMENT
      (Strictly df.iloc[:T+1], No Future Data)             (Evaluated Post-Signal Only)
```

1. **Strict Data Boundary**: At any historical checkpoint $T$, the signal-generation engine receives **only** `df.iloc[:T+1]`.
2. **Physical Isolation**: Future prices ($T+1, \dots, T+H$) are physically absent from the signal generation context. Indicator series, moving averages, RSI, ATR contraction, VSA classifications, P&F box charts, setup scores, and candidate classifications are computed using historical data up to $T$ only.
3. **Future Shock Invariance**: Verified via automated regression test (`test_future_bar_leakage_detection`), which mutates future prices after bar $T$ by $5\times$ and proves that the generated candidate category, composite score, mechanical qualification, and disqualification flags at $T$ remain bitwise identical.
4. **Post-Signal Outcome Evaluation**: Forward returns, Maximum Favorable Excursion (MFE), and Maximum Adverse Excursion (MAE) are measured in a separate outcome layer that accesses only the forward window $[T+1 \dots T+H]$.

---

## 3. Checkpoint & Horizon Methodology
- **Warm-up Period**: $W = 200$ daily bars ($\approx 10$ calendar months) required to ensure stable, mature lookbacks for 40-week WMA, 100 DMA, and multi-month accumulation trading ranges.
- **Checkpoint Stride**: $\text{step} = 5$ daily bars (weekly Friday cadence), providing regular screening snapshots without excessive daily autocorrelation.
- **Checkpoint Range**: From index $T = 199$ through $T \le N - 60$ (ensuring complete 60-day forward return availability).
- **Forward Horizons**:
  - $H = 10$ trading days ($\approx 2$ calendar weeks): Immediate reaction following signal detection.
  - $H = 20$ trading days ($\approx 1$ calendar month): Intermediate swing continuation / markup initiation.
  - $H = 60$ trading days ($\approx 3$ calendar months / 1 quarter): Sustained Wyckoff markup phase outperformance.

---

## 4. Mathematical Return & Excursion Formulas

For each checkpoint bar $T$ and forward horizon $H$:

1. **Forward Percentage Return**:
   $$\text{FwdRet}_{H} = \left(\frac{\text{Close}[T+H] - \text{Close}[T]}{\text{Close}[T]}\right) \times 100\%$$

2. **Maximum Favorable Excursion (MFE %)**:
   $$\text{MFE}_{H} = \left(\frac{\max_{t \in [T+1, T+H]}(\text{High}[t]) - \text{Close}[T]}{\text{Close}[T]}\right) \times 100\%$$

3. **Maximum Adverse Excursion (MAE %)**:
   $$\text{MAE}_{H} = \left(\frac{\min_{t \in [T+1, T+H]}(\text{Low}[t]) - \text{Close}[T]}{\text{Close}[T]}\right) \times 100\%$$

4. **Hit Rate (Win Rate %)**:
   $$\text{WinRate}_{H} = \left(\frac{\sum \mathbb{I}(\text{FwdRet}_{H} > 0.0)}{N_{\text{valid}}}\right) \times 100\%$$

## 5. Cohort Analysis & Baseline Comparisons
Every historical observation is aggregated across multiple analytical groupings:
1. **Universe Equal-Weighted Baseline**: Mean and median forward return of all securities in the universe across the exact same historical checkpoints.
2. **Candidate Categories**:
   - `HIGH_PRIORITY_CANDIDATE`: Mechanically qualified $\land$ Score $\ge 60.0 \land (\text{LPS} \lor \text{SOS} \lor \text{Spring})$.
   - `QUALIFIED_CANDIDATE`: Mechanically qualified $\land$ Score $\ge 40.0$.
   - `WATCHLIST`: Non-disqualified with candidate event or Score $\ge 30.0$.
   - `NO_SETUP`: Remaining non-disqualified securities.
   - `DISQUALIFIED`: Structural red flags present (UTAD, absent accumulation base, failed mechanical filters).
3. **Mechanical Qualification Gate**: `is_mechanically_qualified == True` vs `False`.
4. **Disqualification Gate**: `is_disqualified == True` vs `False`.
5. **Score Tiers**: High ($\ge 60.0$), Medium ($40.0 - 59.9$), Low ($< 40.0$).

---

## 6. Train / Validation / Test Discipline
To eliminate hindsight bias and threshold over-fitting:
- **In-Sample / Development Period**: Checkpoints from `2023-11-01` to `2024-12-31`.
- **Out-of-Sample / Test Period**: Checkpoints from `2025-01-01` to `2026-05-31`.
- **Untouched Out-of-Sample Invariant**: Scoring weights, VSA thresholds, and mechanical gates are never tuned based on out-of-sample test results.

---

## 7. Survivorship Bias Disclosure & Boundary
- **Current Universe Limitation**: The Phase 9B research dataset is constructed from current active NSE EQ listings.
- **Limitation**: Historical validation over 2023–2026 excludes companies delisted or suspended during this period.
- **Mandatory Notice**: All validation manifests and reports prominently state:
  `CURRENT-UNIVERSE HISTORICAL VALIDATION (Subject to Survivorship Bias; for forward triage evaluation only)`.

---

## 8. Output Schemas (`data/validation_results/<YYYYMMDD>/`)

1. **`validation_manifest.json`**: Machine-readable audit file containing run parameters, input snapshot directory, sample sizes, reconciliation equality ($\text{attempted} = \text{success} + \text{failed}$), and survivorship warning.
2. **`signal_events.csv`**: Granular record of every point-in-time signal:
   - `symbol`, `checkpoint_date`, `bar_index`, `close_at_checkpoint`, `candidate_category`, `is_mechanically_qualified`, `composite_score`, `is_disqualified`, `disqualifying_flags`, `most_recent_event`, `numeric_evidence`, `pf_target_price`, `pf_upside_pct`, `fwd_ret_10d`, `fwd_ret_20d`, `fwd_ret_60d`, `mfe_10d`, `mfe_20d`, `mfe_60d`, `mae_10d`, `mae_20d`, `mae_60d`, `period_split`, `tradingview_url_daily`, `tradingview_url_weekly`.
3. **`category_performance.csv`**: Aggregated performance metrics by candidate category and baseline across all horizons (count, win rate %, mean return %, median return %, standard deviation %, 25th/75th percentiles, mean MFE %, mean MAE %).
4. **`score_band_performance.csv`**: Forward performance broken down by score tiers.
5. **`in_sample_vs_out_sample.csv`**: Comparative side-by-side performance across temporal splits.
6. **`failures.csv`**: Isolated point-in-time exceptions.
